A portfolio is a strange format. It shows you the two or three projects that
worked, tidied up, with the dead ends cropped out. Useful for someone deciding
whether to work with me — less useful for anyone trying to learn the same
things I'm learning.

<!--more-->

So this part of the site is for the rest of it. Half working notebook, half
public record.

## What goes here

Roughly three kinds of thing:

- **Working notes.** What I tried in ArcGIS or QGIS this week, what broke, and
  what I'd do differently. Mostly hydrology and land suitability at the moment.
- **Studio notes.** What's on the table under Bunya Art, and the slow discovery
  that pouring ink and modelling drainage are closer to the same problem than
  they look.
- **Opinions about maps.** Occasional, and usually about legibility — whether
  the person the map is *for* can actually read it.

## What doesn't

Anything I can't say publicly about the people I work with. The community and
employment side of my work is other people's business, not content.
